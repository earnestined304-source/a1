Privacy Policy

Learn about WisdomSchool's data privacy, student record protections, and FERPA/GDPR compliance.

1. Institutional Privacy Commitment

WisdomSchool Academy ('WisdomSchool', 'we', 'our') is deeply committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of personal information collected from students, parents, alumni, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details our data collection, usage, and safeguarding practices.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly through admissions forms, visit scheduling inquiries, and communications with our faculty deans, including names, contact numbers, email addresses, and educational background details.

3. How We Use Academic Data

Collected data is used strictly for admissions evaluation, educational administrative communications, academic progress tracking, and institutional announcements. We never sell, lease, or monetize personal information.

4. Security Safeguards

We deploy robust physical, administrative, and technical safeguards, including SSL encryption and restricted server access, to protect personal data against unauthorized disclosure.

5. Contact Data Privacy Officer

For inquiries regarding personal records, contact us at: 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States or call +1-888-777-5845.
