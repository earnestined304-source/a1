Cookie Policy

Information about the transparent use of technical cookies on the WisdomSchool website.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser to facilitate seamless site navigation, remember user preferences (such as light/dark scholarly themes), and collect aggregate performance metrics.

2. Types of Cookies We Utilize

Essential Cookies: Required for site security, navigation, and theme state storage.
Analytics Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics (Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) to understand website visitor engagement and optimize academic navigation.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

Visitors may modify their browser settings at any time to refuse or delete cookies without losing access to core reading materials on our website.
