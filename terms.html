Terms of Enrollment

Terms and academic policies governing enrollment, campus conduct, and intellectual standards at WisdomSchool.

1. Acceptance of Academic Terms

By accessing the WisdomSchool website or enrolling in academy programs, parents, students, and visitors agree to abide by these Terms of Enrollment and our institutional standards of intellectual virtue and conduct.

2. Code of Intellectual Honesty

WisdomSchool upholds uncompromising standards of academic integrity. Plagiarism, uncredited attribution, or misrepresentation of original scholarship is strictly prohibited across all coursework and senior thesis defenses.

3. Intellectual Assets and Courseware

All curriculum syllabi, lecture manuscripts, and institutional publications remain the scholarly asset and intellectual ownership of WisdomSchool Academy.

4. Disciplinary Standards & Conduct

The academy reserves the right to suspend or dismiss any student whose behavior violates our code of mutual respect, safety, or academic dedication.
